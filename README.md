# VMG – V Modelines Generator

https://hardcade-system.com/img/Vmodelines.png

**VMG (V Modelines Generator)** is a video modeline generator developed by HardCade for retro gaming enthusiasts and users working with custom video modes and CRT displays.

VMG allows you to create precise video configurations, with a refresh rate adjustment resolution of up to **0.001 Hz**. It supports low and high resolutions, from **240p to 1080p**, including **15 kHz** configurations for CRT televisions and arcade monitors.

## Features

- **High-precision modeline generation:** fine-tune refresh rates with an adjustment resolution of up to 0.001 Hz.
- **Wide resolution support:** generate modelines for low- and high-resolution video modes, from 240p to 1080p.
- **15 kHz CRT support:** configure video modes for compatible CRT televisions and arcade monitors.
- **Pixel clock overclocking:** increase the pixel clock frequency without altering the structure of the modelines.
- **Soft15kHz included:** provided primarily for use with older-generation NVIDIA graphics cards.
- **CRT Emudriver compatibility:** designed to work with compatible ATI/AMD Radeon graphics cards using CRT Emudriver.


**Note:** When launching VMG, Windows will ask for permission to make changes to your system. This is normal and required for Soft15kHz configuration. Simply click **Yes** to continue.

VMG is intended for users configuring custom video modes for retro gaming, CRT displays, and arcade setups.

Compatibility depends on your graphics card, drivers, operating system, and display. Always verify that your hardware supports the selected video modes before applying them.
