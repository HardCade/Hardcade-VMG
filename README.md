**VMG – V Modelines Generator**

VMG is a modeline generator designed to create precise video configurations, with a frequency adjustment resolution of up to **0.001 Hz**. It supports both low and high resolutions, as well as the configuration of **15 kHz** CRT displays.

VMG also allows for increasing the pixel clock frequency without altering the structure of the modelines, providing greater flexibility when fine-tuning video modes.

The software comes bundled with **Soft15kHz**, primarily for use with older-generation NVIDIA graphics cards. It is also compatible with **CRT Emudriver** and **ATI Radeon** graphics cards.
